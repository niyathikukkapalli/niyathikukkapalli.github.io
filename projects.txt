After introducing the basics of p-adic analysis, including why Q<sub>p</sub> is the completion of Q, this paper shows that the exponential and logarithm remain inverses, with intuition drawn from metric spaces and topology. The core is an inductive-based proof of Dwork's Lemma, used to prove the integrality of the Artin–Hasse Exponential.
